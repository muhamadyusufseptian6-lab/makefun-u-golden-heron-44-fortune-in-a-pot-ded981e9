[Fortune in a Pot](https://make.fun/competitions/u-golden-heron-44-fortune-in-a-pot-ded981e9)
bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

## The fortune

> I watch from my sunny sill: you will spill your coffee near me, and I will drink it and grow a leaf by Friday.

The plant speaks for itself: it predicts the keeper's spilled coffee, then
answers with a small wonder — drinking it and growing a brand-new leaf by
Friday. Patient, green, rooted, and watching from the window.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only, deterministic) writes the fortune
to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` — a single line of at most 120 characters.

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out node main.ts
cat /tmp/out/entry.txt
```
